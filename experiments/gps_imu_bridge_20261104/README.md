# GPS/IMU pose bridge: passive ground probe

The unmodified simulator was started with seed 123 and NullRHI, and `bridge_observed_v1.py` subscribed only to GPS and IMU and published a new diagnostic pose topic. No flight controller or debug truth was connected. Raw GPS callback timing, source stamps, IMU age, GPS position and IMU orientation are in `samples.jsonl`; the pool manifest and full simulator log are preserved. All 121 GPS callbacks in 12 wall-clock seconds passed the bridge's finite, quaternion, freshness and source-stamp skew checks. Maximum absolute source-stamp separation was approximately 12 ms and maximum IMU arrival age about 10.1 ms. Source stamp differences are not end-to-end latency and the test says nothing about flight dynamics, the pose frame or orientation equivalence to the beta controller.

The observed script is retained byte-for-byte separately from subsequent development. This is a feasibility check, not a validated state estimator, a competition navigation system, or evidence that stage 4 was reached. Further offline frame verification and bounded controlled flight are needed before the beta controller can safely consume the new topic.
